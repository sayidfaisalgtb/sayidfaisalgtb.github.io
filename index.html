About

I studied education at the Institute of Education and Research, University of Dhaka, and somewhere along the way storytelling took over. Most of my work now sits at the crossing point of the two: documentary films made for NGOs and INGOs, built on the same research instincts I picked up in my degree.

I have filmed in tea gardens in Rangamati, school kitchens in Lalmonirhat, and flood-prone villages in Pabna, always working closely with field teams to get consent, comfort, and honest stories on camera. Outside client work, I run Lens Flare Studio and stay active in youth and environmental volunteering across Dhaka.
